Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 30, 2026

Tradeless ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. Tradeless is designed as a local-first, API-driven trading interface. We do not store your trading credentials, API secrets, or transaction history on any external servers.

1. Local Encryption & Storage

All broker API keys, API secrets, session tokens, and user settings remain 100% encrypted on your local device's hardware-backed secure storage. They are never transmitted to Tradeless servers.

2. Direct Broker Connections

When you execute trades or view live market data, Tradeless sends requests directly from your device to your broker's official API endpoints. No intermediary servers participate in your transactions.

3. Data Collection

We do not collect personal identify information, order books, portfolio balances, or financial logs. Any app analytics collected (if enabled) are strictly aggregated and anonymous crash telemetry to improve performance.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact support at a4avishkar@gmail.com.
